Platform overview

What a full admin does

  • Approve restaurants (shops) and delivery partners before they go live.
  • Manage catalog listings, commission (e.g. 15–30% capped), and payouts after customer payments settle.
  • Monitor Orders — OrderID, UserID, Status — and GPS/live tracking in production.
  • Handle cancellations/refunds (5–7 business days on major platforms) and disputes.
  • Dashboards: GMV, active users, delivery SLA, marketing spend.
